ABOUT US

Cultivating Curious Minds

Born from a seed of wonder, AAIS sprouted to nurture blossoming minds in an enriching, child-centered early learning environment. Today, our roots weave a vibrant community of dedicated educators, supportive families, and curious little explorers. Together, we watch laughter bloom, imaginations take flight, and tiny caterpillars transform into confident butterflies, ready to embrace the world beyond.

Our Vision

At AAIS our vision is to provide a vigorous curriculum that empowers students for personal growth and lifelong learning.

We cultivate resilience and essential 21st-century skills, preparing them to thrive and positively impact global and local communities.

Our Mission

Our academic and extracurricular programs are carefully crafted to be engaging and meaningful.

We are passionate about ensuring our students become capable, responsible, and confident individuals.

A Vigorous Curriculum

We nurture the whole child through experiential education, encouraging emotional growth, creativity, and critical thinking. Our classrooms extend beyond walls into nature and community.

Nature-Based Education

Our environment encourages outdoor exploration and environmental awareness. Children develop a deep respect for the Earth through hands-on learning experiences.

Intercultural Community

We celebrate diversity and global perspectives, fostering empathy and understanding among students.

Future-Ready Students

We equip students with resilience, confidence, and essential 21st-century skills to prepare them for meaningful futures both locally and globally.
