Enrollment Applications

When should we start an application to enrol at AAIS?

Please contact us as soon as you think that a move to Lombok may be in your future. Admissions are reviewed on a rolling basis. We suggest gathering your materials early to complete your application as soon as possible.

When will applications not be processed in a given year?

Applications may pause during school holidays or when classes have reached capacity. Please contact our admissions office for updated availability.

How compatible is AAIS curriculum with other international schools?

Our curriculum is aligned with international standards, making transitions smooth for students moving between schools locally and globally.

Do you offer extracurricular programs?

Yes, we offer a variety of extracurricular activities including arts, sports, environmental programs, and cultural workshops.
