Our Programs

Our program blends play-based and nature-based activities, ensuring age-appropriate experiences that foster holistic child development. Through engaging play and exploration, we aim to cultivate potential, enhance problem-solving, and instill a love for learning. The incorporation of nature enriches the environment, connecting children with the world around them. The program emphasizes not only academic skills but also essential life skills, shaping well-rounded individuals for future success. Don’t be surprised to witness our school mantra in action daily: At Anak Alam School, we care, we show respect, we love to grow, and we always try our best!

Early Years

Our Early Years Program is a Play based curriculum anchored in hands-on nature experiences, learning by doing and working with and caring about others to build positive relationships and social-emotional thinking skills.Teaching and learning is student centred and inquiry based with our literacy and numeracy outcomes from the Australian Curriculum Learning Framework.

Community Engagement

Students, staff and families engage with the community in meaningful service. We embrace the Oath for Compassionate Helpers with the core commitment of:“ I will listen carefully and seek ways to empower others while never doing for others what they have the capacity to do for themselves.”

Anak Alam IS runs after school classes for local children to learn English and be capable of taking action for sustainable solutions in the community. The management team is committed to supporting the regional education department in training teachers in innovative pedagogy, through internships, workshops and lectures.
